# Interview-friendly findings and metric cautions

- **Market concentration:** Delhi 16, Chennai 15, Jaipur 11 registered customers. Not proof of revenue concentration.
- **Customer frequency:** 84 observed purchasers, 16 non-purchasers, 58 repeat purchasers (two or more orders).
- **Category reach:** Electronics reaches 79 registered customers; other categories may overlap.
- **Product frequency:** Product 7 appeared in 78 order-detail lines; not a measure of inventory turnover.
- **Sales and AOV:** September 2023 revenue 2,927,000 (dataset monetary units); December 2023 AOV 132,095.24. February 2024 is incomplete.
- **Acquisition:** First observed orders taper across the observation window; without campaign and traffic data no causal claim is justified.

**Recommendations:** retention analysis, replenishment investigation with stock data, category-specific discovery tests and monthly dashboarding with full-month comparisons.
